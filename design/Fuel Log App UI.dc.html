Fuel Log MPG & CAR EXPENSES
Fuel Log: MPG & Car Expenses · v1 · US / UK 1a

Numbers first, chrome last

Fourteen screens in flow order: splash, first-launch country and currency, units, first vehicle, then the garage — which is the homepage every later screen returns to — vehicle, the two entry screens, statistics, month history and month detail, vehicle settings, app settings. Colors are the PDF Toolkit set — yellow, ink, paper grey — but the layout is its own: hairline rules instead of cards, oversized tabular figures, yellow reserved for the one action per screen. Defaults are miles, US gallons, MPG and dollars; units and currency are per vehicle, and mixed currencies are shown as subtotals, never converted.
